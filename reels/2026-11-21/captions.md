# 2026-11-21 · Do not despair of Allah's mercy (Qur'an 39:53–54)

## Instagram @ibadah.daily
Think your sins are too many for Allah to forgive? 🤍

Qur'an 39:53–54 (Surah Az-Zumar)
Translation: Saheeh International · Explanation: Tafsir Ibn Kathir

"Do not despair of the mercy of Allāh. Indeed, Allāh forgives all sins."
Ibn Kathir: this is a call to every sinner to repent. Whoever turns back to Allah is forgiven, even if the sins are like the foam of the sea. The door of repentance and mercy is wide.
Save this for the day you feel too far, and send it to someone who thinks it's too late.
